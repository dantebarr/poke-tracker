So you're the new Ranger. Good. Sit down a moment.

This reserve doesn't run itself. Fences, water, records — there's a share of it with your name on it every day, and I'll expect it done.

Here's what nobody tells you on the first day: the Pokémon watch. Do the work and one will wander over, decide you're worth the trouble, and stay. It'll help. It won't be caught, and it won't be told what to do.

Keep at it and you'll know it well enough to write its entry properly — and it'll trust you enough to change in front of you. Let it down often enough and it wanders off again. It remembers you either way.

That's the whole job. Go on.

SAFARI ZONE · RESERVE OFFICE

So you're the new Ranger. Good. Sit down a moment.

This reserve doesn't run itself. Fences, water, records — there's a share of it with your name on it every single day, and I'll expect it done.

Here's the part nobody tells you on the first day: the Pokémon watch. Do the work and one of them will wander over, decide you're worth the trouble, and stay. It'll help. It won't be caught, and it won't be told what to do.

Keep at it and you'll come to know that Pokémon well enough to write its entry properly — and it'll trust you enough to change in front of you. Let it down often enough and it'll wander off again. It'll remember you either way.

That's the whole job. Go on.

Shown once, on a Ranger's first day.
